# Hush Line Board of Advisors

Over the year, we'll cover the journey of industry-specific whistleblowing and map our review of Hush Line to those phases.

**The journey:**

Understanding the Law > Making a Decision to Disclose > Going Through It > After Disclosure

---

## Q1 — Understanding the Law

### Live Question
“What laws and regulations do whistleblowers in your sector need to understand (for example, HIPAA, SEC/FINRA, KYC, procurement/confidentiality rules), and how did those rules shape your own disclosure journey? Did you know about these rules when you started your journey?”

### Take-Home Question
“What legal misunderstanding would you warn a new user about before they send their first message?”

### Flow Review While Walking Through App
- First-time user experience
- User loads the app and sees the blocking modal
- Review the information
- Review the emergency exit button

### Questions to Ask
1. We're showing a few high-level tips when someone first lands on the site.
2. What should be there, and what shouldn't?
3. What website should the emergency exit link to that is most benign?
4. Any other feedback?

### Expected Outputs

**Live question**
- Relevant laws for represented industries

**Take-home question**
- Understand best practices for disclosure planning

**Flow review**
- Learn how to make the landing more helpful

## Q2 — Making a Decision to Disclose

### Live Question
“What nearly stopped you from disclosing, and what finally pushed you to move forward?”

### Take-Home Question
“What is one decision a whistleblower should slow down before making?”

### Flow Review While Walking Through App
- Directory view and discovery of recipients
- Verified vs non-verified users
- Reading recipient profiles
- Deciding who to contact
- Submitting a question

### Questions to Ask
1. Does the directory help someone think clearly before acting, or push them to act too quickly?
2. What information helps someone decide who is safe to contact?
3. What information might create false confidence?
4. What signals would help a user pause and think before sending a message?
5. What decision support is missing at this stage?

### Expected Outputs

**Live question**
- Real decision blockers
- Emotional and practical hesitation points
- Triggers that move users from uncertainty to action

**Take-home question**
- Decisions that should be delayed
- Guidance for “decide before you disclose” resources

**Flow review**
- Improvements to directory context
- Safer recipient selection signals
- Decision-support UX additions

## Q4 — After Disclosure

### Live Question
“What surprised you most about what happened after disclosure?”

### Take-Home Question
“If someone restarted today, what would they do differently in the first week after disclosing?”

### Flow Review While Walking Through App
- Ongoing conversation interface
- Notification expectations
- Guidance after sending first message
- User uncertainty while waiting
- Continued safety behavior

### Questions to Ask
1. What support should appear immediately after submission?
2. What risks begin only after disclosure is sent?
3. What reassurance helps without creating false expectations?
4. What behaviors should the platform discourage at this stage?
5. What resources should appear automatically?

### Expected Outputs

**Live question**
- Early retaliation or exposure patterns
- Post-disclosure stress points
- Survival needs users experience

**Take-home question**
- First-week survival guidance
- Stabilization best practices

**Flow review**
- Post-submission guidance design
- Safety reminders
- Expectation-setting language
- Feature priorities for ongoing support
